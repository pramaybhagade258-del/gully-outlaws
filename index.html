The Story

FROM THE CHAWL
TO THE TOP

Set in the raw, electric streets of 1990s Mumbai, Gully Outlaws drops you into the shoes of Raju — a small-time street kid from Dharavi with nothing but nerve and a dream.

Navigate the labyrinthine gullies, forge alliances with rival gangs, bribe corrupt constables, and climb the underworld ladder one chai-stall deal at a time.

Every decision echoes. Every gully has a memory. Will you become a legend — or just another ghost of the streets?

🏙️

Living Mumbai

A hand-crafted recreation of 90s Bombay — local trains, vada pav stalls, monsoon floods, and Ganesh utsav processions.

⚔️

Street Combat System

Fluid brawling inspired by real desi fighting styles — belt swings, bamboo sticks, and raw knuckle action.

🎵

Original Desi Soundtrack

From tapori beats to atmospheric bhajan remixes — music that breathes the city's soul.

📸 HOW TO ADD YOUR REAL SCREENSHOTS

After hosting on GitHub Pages — follow these 5 steps

01

ADD YOUR IMAGES TO THE REPO

Create an images/ folder in the root of your GitHub repo and drop your screenshots in — name them screenshot_01.jpg, screenshot_02.jpg, etc. (or any name you like).

02

REPLACE THE PLACEHOLDER DIV WITH AN <img> TAG

In index.html, find each <div class="shot-placeholder"> block and replace it with:
<img src="images/screenshot_01.jpg" alt="Dharavi Horizon" style="width:100%;display:block;" />

03

COMMIT AND PUSH

Save the file, then in your terminal:

git add . git commit -m "Add real screenshots" git push origin main
04

ENABLE GITHUB PAGES

Go to your repo → Settings → Pages → under "Source" select main branch, folder / (root) → click Save. GitHub will give you a live URL like https://yourusername.github.io/gully-outlaws/

05

WAIT ~60 SECONDS & VISIT YOUR SITE

GitHub Pages takes about a minute to deploy after the first push. Refresh the provided URL and your full site — screenshots included — will be live for the world to see.

Game Features

WHAT MAKES THE GULLY

🗺️

OPEN WORLD MUMBAI

35 sq km of hand-crafted 90s Bombay — from Dharavi slums to Malabar Hill bungalows. Every gully, every staircase, every rooftop is explorable.

01
👊

TAPORI COMBAT

A fluid, physics-based brawl system inspired by real street fighting. Improvised weapons, environmental takedowns, and desi wrestling moves.

02
🎭

BRANCHING STORY

60+ hours of narrative with 4 major endings. Your choices ripple through the chawl — alliances form, betrayals sting, and the streets remember.

03
🌧️

DYNAMIC WEATHER

Mumbai's legendary monsoon is a game mechanic. Rain floods shortcuts, cops take shelter, and rival gangs use the downpour to settle scores.

04
🎵

DESI OST

80-track original soundtrack — tapori hip-hop, qawwali basslines, Bollywood-noir strings, and local folk percussion that shifts with the time of day.

05
🤝

GANG SYSTEM

Build your own crew. Recruit characters from the streets, assign roles, and manage loyalty. Every gang member has a backstory, a weakness, and ambitions of their own.
